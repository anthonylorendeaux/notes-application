## Workshop

**Context** :

You are looking for a land on which to build a new family home. You have already established a short list of specifications:

- Land : 500m2 mini close to your job (less than 10km)
- House: 120m2 mini with 4 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms.
- You want a vegetable garden (10m2 mini)

You will need to move into the family home within the next 2 years as the landlord of your current flat has decided to sell it.

Your savings are up to 100 000 euros but you do not know how much you can borrow.

Your best friend is an architect and can help you with the house drawing.

**Business case** :

The purpose of this project is to :

- Find a suitable land and build a new family home within the next 2 years.

- The project will include finding a land that meets the specifications of being 500m2 minimum and located within 10km of the job.
- The house will be a minimum of 120m2 with 4 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms
- There will be a vegetable garden of at least 10m2.
- The budget for the project is 100,000 euros, with the possibility of additional funds through borrowing.
