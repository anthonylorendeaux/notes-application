I choose the subject: "The Ethics of UX (User Experience): Why UX is a problem?". Because it's a topic closely related to my PFE, which is the development of a web application.

First, let's explain a bit what User Experience is. The User Experience (UX) Design is the process that design teams use to create products that provide meaningful and relevant experiences for their users. UX design involves designing the entire product procurement and integration process, including aspects of branding, design, usability, and functionality.

The problem with user experience can be the ethic of the web designer behind it. Because the designer is following psychological analysis and facts to create the design, he can also be tempted to try to manipulate our senses to make us do what he wants to do. For example, big applications like Instagram, Facebook or Tiktok are made to makes us consume the content without seeing the time pass. We can also take the example of the red dot in our applications on our smartphone which are made to always makes us click because the red colour is influencing our brains by telling us "Alert".

There is also new trends developing among the web designer, about making application more ethics without trying to influence the user and created a more mindful experience.

## Plan of the Ted Talk

My plan for this ted talk will be the following:

- What is the User Experience
- The history of the User Experience
- The ethics problem of the UX
- New ways of designing

## Text

Hello everyone,

Today, I would like to talk to you about the User Experience or UX for short. My plan for this TED talk will be the following: First, I will define what the User Experience is, then I will discuss its history, the ethics problem of the UX, and finally, new ways of designing.

So, what is the User Experience? The User Experience is the overall experience a user has when interacting with a product, service or system. It encompasses all aspects of the user's interaction, including usability, accessibility, functionality, design, and branding. Essentially, the UX is all about creating products and services that meet the needs of users and provide a positive experience.

The history of the User Experience is a fascinating one. The term "User Experience" was first coined by Don Norman in the 1990s, although the concept has been around for much longer. In fact, the idea of designing products for users dates back to the 1940s when the field of human factors engineering emerged. Human factors engineering focused on designing products that were easier and more intuitive for people to use.

As UX design has grown in popularity, so too have the ethical concerns surrounding it. One of the most significant problems in the UX industry is the issue of user privacy. Many companies collect and use data about their users without their knowledge or consent, which raises questions about the ethics of UX design. Designers must be aware of these issues and ensure that their designs are ethical and respectful of user privacy.

Finally, I want to talk to you about new ways of designing for the User Experience. One of the most exciting developments in UX design is the emergence of conversational interfaces. With conversational interfaces, users can interact with products and services using natural language, making the experience more intuitive and less frustrating. Another new development is the use of augmented and virtual reality in UX design, which allows designers to create immersive experiences that can transport users to new worlds.

In conclusion, the User Experience is a crucial aspect of product and service design, and designers must be aware of its history, ethical concerns, and new developments. By prioritizing the user experience and designing products that meet user needs and provide a positive experience, we can create a world that is more accessible and enjoyable for everyone. Thank you.
